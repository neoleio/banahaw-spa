Our Story

A name borrowed from the mountain that has always meant healing

Mt. Banahaw has long been regarded by Laguna's communities as a place of restoration. We took its name as a promise: every visit to our clinic in Calamba City should leave you feeling the way pilgrims describe coming down from the mountain — lighter, calmer, and well.

Banahaw Spa was founded to give working professionals, students, local families, and visiting tourists a dependable space for therapeutic massage and genuine rest. Every therapist on our team is TESDA-certified and trained in both Western techniques and traditional Filipino hilot-informed bodywork.

  • Licensed and continuously trained massage therapists
  • Hospital-grade sanitation between every single session
  • A calm, private environment designed for genuine rest
